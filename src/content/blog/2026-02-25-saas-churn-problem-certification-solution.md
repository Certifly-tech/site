## Why Certification Works Where Product Tours Fail

### 1. Intrinsic vs. Extrinsic Motivation
Product tours say “click here to remove this modal.” Certification says “master this to advance your career.” One creates annoyance, the other creates ambition.

### 2. Social Proof & Professional Identity
Certified users:

- Mention them in job interviews  
- Reference them in client proposals

No user has ever bragged about completing your product tour.

### 3. The Sunk Cost Fallacy Works in Your Favor
When users invest 10–40 hours earning a certification, they’re psychologically committed. Walking away means admitting those hours were wasted — something humans are notoriously bad at.
